Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Knyaw Bible ("the app") is made by Ehblut Htoo ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not track you, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

1. What the app keeps

  • Your documents, songs, notes, highlights, plans and settings. They are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. We cannot read them: they are not kept on our servers.
  • Sign in with Apple. If you sign in, Apple gives the app your name, an email address (or Apple's private relay address, if you choose to hide yours) and a user identifier. The app uses these to show who you are and to share documents with other people.

2. Sharing

If you share a document, the people you invite can see it and what you write in it. This happens through iCloud. You control who has access and can stop sharing at any time.

3. Calendar

If you allow it, the app reads and adds events to your calendar so that your services and documents appear there. Calendar data stays on your device and in your iCloud. You can turn this off in System Settings.

4. Audio downloads

If you download Bible or hymn audio, the app connects to audio.knyawbible.com to fetch the files. The app does not send any personal information when it does this.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

6. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not see or store your payment details.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete it. Deleting a document removes it from your devices and from iCloud. To delete your account in the app, open Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete Account. To delete everything the app keeps in iCloud, open System Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage ▸ Knyaw Bible ▸ Delete Documents & Data.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.

10. Contact
