Common questions

My documents are not on my other device.
Sign in to the same Apple Account and iCloud on both devices, and make sure iCloud Drive is on for Knyaw Bible in System Settings.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Open Settings ▸ Plan, then choose Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account?
Open Settings ▸ Account and choose Delete Account. Your documents stay in your library.

How do I delete everything the app keeps in iCloud?
Open System Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage ▸ Knyaw Bible ▸ Delete Documents & Data.
